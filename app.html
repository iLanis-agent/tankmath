Why "40 gallons" is not 40 showers

Tanks are sold by volume, but only about 70% stays truly hot - the bottom layer mixes cold the moment you draw. A shower is a blend: at 40 C from a 60 C tank with 15 C inlet water, 56% of every litre comes from the tank. Recovery is the fuel's whole story: gas reheats roughly 2.6x faster than a standard electric element, which is why the same size tank feels bigger on gas. The cheapest upgrade is not a bigger tank - it is a smaller flow head and one less minute.
